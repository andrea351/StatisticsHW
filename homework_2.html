Homework 2: hashing, key derivation and cryptographic primitives

From a wallet seed to an Ethereum address

A wallet such as MetaMask turns twelve words into keys and addresses through six standards, each solving a different problem. This page runs the whole pipeline in your browser with primitives written from scratch, checks it against the official test vectors and measures its statistical behaviour.
